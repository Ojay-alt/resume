Who I Am

About Me

My name is Glory O. Uchola, an experienced administrative and executive assistant with a strong background in office operations, HR coordination, and virtual support. I hold a Master of Public Health (2025), with certifications in data analysis and IT support.

I am currently attending the ALX Foundational Program in Data Analysis, where I am learning to clean, analyze, and visualize data to uncover insights. My professional journey combines years of managing people, processes, and information with a growing passion for data computing.

I am building a brand centered on accuracy, efficiency, and impact, using data to help organizations make better decisions and solve global challenges like food security, health access, and sustainability.

Why Data Analysis?

I chose data analysis because I have always worked with data manually: tracking attendance, compiling reports, managing employee records, and organizing spreadsheets. Now I want to take it further by using analysis to understand problems and design stronger solutions.

My Vision

To become a data analyst who can stand at the intersection of administration, public health, and technology, using data computing to solve global problems, especially in Africa's development sectors.
